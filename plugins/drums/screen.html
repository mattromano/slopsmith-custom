Drums

Settings for the Drum Highway. They're saved on this computer, and each player in multiplayer keeps their own. The ⚙ button in the player has the same settings while you play.

Difficulty & assists

Default difficulty
Songs open at this difficulty. Songs without it play Expert. While playing: click the badge, or press D / Shift+D.
Auto kick
The game plays the kick for you (pedal hits are ignored and it isn't scored). Choose which difficulties it's on for.
Auto cymbals
The game plays hi-hat, ride and crash notes for you, so you only play snare, toms (and kick).
Hit timing
How early or late a hit can be and still count (3D view). Relaxed is easier on a laggy setup.
Pro cymbals
On: cymbals and toms of the same colour are separate notes. Off: either pad of a colour counts.

Drum sounds

Kit
Sound played when you hit a pad.
Volume
0% turns the drum sounds off (use this if your kit module makes its own sound). Sound from the computer adds some delay.

Your kit (MIDI)

Input
Your drum kit is picked automatically when it's plugged in.
MIDI channel
Only listen to one channel (most kits send drums on 10).
Pad tester
Hit your pads: the lane each one counts as lights up and the kit sound plays.
Pad mapping
Click Learn next to a lane, then hit the pad you want for it.

Display & controls

View
3D drum track, or the flat 2D lanes. Auto uses 3D when the computer supports it.
Input offset
Raise it if your hits register late, lower it if they register early (3D view).
ms
Keyboard drumming
3D view: B kick, F red, J/K/L yellow/blue/green, Shift or U/I/O for cymbals, Enter for star power.
2D lane layout
Phase Shift: 8 lanes (hi-hat, snare, 3 toms, crash, ride, kick). Rock Band: 7 lanes.
2D lane labels
2D hit counter
Count hits and misses in the 2D view (the 3D view always scores).
